Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-20

What we collect

Contact details you give us (email or Telegram account), the domains you register for your key, and usage statistics of the widget: number of views per key and month. To avoid counting the same visitor twice, the widget server briefly keeps the visitor IP address in memory (up to 30 minutes); it is not stored in our database.

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising trackers in the widget and do not sell or share personal data with third parties, except payment processors that handle your payments.

Payments

Payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not receive or store your card details.

Retention and deletion

We keep account data while your key is active and for up to 12 months after. You can ask us to delete your data at any time: support@dghostmarket.uz.
