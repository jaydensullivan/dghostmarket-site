FAQ

How is this different from Steam images?

Steam has one image per skin. We render the exact item — its float (wear) and pattern (artwork placement) — in 3D from every side.

How close is it to the game?

Models and finishes are built from CS2 game files, and we continuously compare them with CSFloat and Steam. Some rare finishes are still in progress — check them on your own items during the trial.

Do you hand out model files?

No. Models are displayed only inside the widget on your domains; files are not downloadable or transferred.

Do you offer an image or video API?

Ready-made images and videos via API are in development. If you need them, let us know your format and we will notify you at launch.
