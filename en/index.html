How a deal works

The item moves directly between Steam accounts, while the platform watches the deadlines and holds the payment until the deal is complete.

🛒 For buyers

  1. Pick a skinA catalog with filters by weapon, quality, float and price.
  2. Pay for the orderWith a method from the app. The platform holds the money — the seller doesn't get it yet.
  3. Receive the itemThe seller sends a Steam trade within 24 hours. If not — you get a refund.
  4. Confirm receiptAccept the trade in Steam and tap “Received”. Something wrong — open a dispute.

💼 For sellers

  1. Get verifiedPhone number via Telegram, a document photo and a selfie — once, before your first listing.
  2. List an itemStraight from your Steam inventory: photo, float and stickers are filled in automatically.
  3. Transfer within 24 hoursAfter payment, send the Steam trade and mark it as “Sent”.
  4. Get paid8 days after payment, the amount minus 5% lands on your balance. Card withdrawals are free.

Deal safety

Platform rules protect both sides: the buyer and the seller.

Verified sellers

Passport or ID, a selfie with the document and a phone number confirmed via Telegram. An admin makes the decision.

Protected payment

Money is held for 8 days from payment — longer than Steam allows a trade to be reversed. Only then does it go to the seller.

Strict deadlines

24 hours to transfer the item. Miss it — the order is cancelled and the buyer is refunded.

Disputes

If the item doesn't arrive, the buyer opens a dispute. The payout is frozen until the admins sort it out.

Anti-fraud

The system looks for linked accounts, reused numbers and suspicious deals. Stolen items mean a ban and frozen funds.

Clear history

Every step is recorded: payment, transfer, confirmation, payout. The history is always available in the app.

FAQ

What if the seller doesn't transfer the item?

If within 24 hours of payment the seller hasn't marked the transfer and the item isn't in your Steam inventory, the order is cancelled automatically and you get your money back.

Why does the seller get paid after 8 days?

Steam allows a trade to be reversed within 7 days. The platform holds the payment a little longer so the buyer isn't left without the item, and the seller isn't left without the money.

Do buyers need to get verified?

No. Buyers just sign in with Telegram and add their Steam trade link. Only sellers get verified.

Is the platform affiliated with Valve or Steam?

No. DGhostMarket is an independent platform. Items move via standard Steam trades between users' accounts.

How do I contact support?

Email support@dghostmarket.uz — we reply within 24 hours. For a specific deal, it's easiest to write from the app.
