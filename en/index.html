FAQ

FAQ

What if the seller doesn't transfer the item?

If within 24 hours of payment the seller hasn't marked the transfer and the item isn't in your Steam inventory, the order is cancelled automatically and you get your money back.

Why does the seller get paid after 8 days?

Steam allows a trade to be reversed within 7 days. The platform holds the payment a little longer so the buyer isn't left without the item, and the seller isn't left without the money.

Do buyers need to get verified?

Not for regular purchases: just sign in with Telegram and add your Steam trade link. For a large purchase, or once a new account hits its limit, the platform will ask you to verify your identity. Sellers are verified before their first listing.

Can I pay with someone else's card?

No. When paying by transfer you enter the cardholder's name, and you can only pay from your own card. A payment from someone else's card is declined and refunded to the sender's card.

How and when are refunds made?

The same way you paid: to your balance right away, to a card within 3 banking days of the refund decision. Refunds are never sent to other cards or accounts.

Is the platform affiliated with Valve or Steam?

No. DGhostMarket is an independent platform. Items move via standard Steam trades between users' accounts.

How do I contact support?

Message @dghostmarketsupportbot on Telegram or email support@dghostmarket.uz — we reply within 24 hours. For a specific deal, it's easiest to write from the app.
